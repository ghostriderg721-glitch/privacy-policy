Privacy Policy — Mahan AI

Ruffian Tech · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Mahan AI is an Android assistant app developed by Ruffian Tech ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app does with your data. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legal language.

The short version: Mahan AI stores your settings, chats and automation history on your own device. When you ask it to perform a task, the screen content it needs to understand is sent to an AI provider to decide the next action. We do not run an account system, we do not sell data, and we do not keep a copy of your screen content on our servers.

1. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone and is not uploaded anywhere:

  • Chat history with the assistant
  • Automation recipes and task history
  • App settings, sleep schedule, blocked-app list
  • Saved contacts you add for auto-reply
  • Crash logs, written to app-private storage

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear it from the app's settings at any time.

2. Accessibility Service — what it does and why

Mahan AI uses Android's Accessibility Service. This is the same system feature screen readers use, and it is the only way an app can read what is on screen and tap for you.

What it is used for: when you give Mahan AI a task such as "search for X in the gallery and share it on WhatsApp", the service reads the visible on-screen elements (button labels, text fields) so it can decide where to tap.

What it does not do: it does not run in the background reading your screen when you have not started a task. Automation only runs after you explicitly start it, and stops when the task ends or when you press the stop button.

Where the screen data goes: to understand a screen, a compact list of visible element labels is sent to an AI provider (see section 4). The list contains text labels such as "Send", "Search", "WhatsApp" and their screen coordinates. It is sent only for the duration of the task.

3. Other permissions we request

  • Notifications access — used by the auto-reply feature to detect an incoming message so it can draft a reply.
  • Microphone — only while you are using voice input. Audio is processed to text and is not stored on our servers.
  • Internet — needed to reach the AI service and to load ads.
  • Display over other apps — draws the small floating control panel and the stop button during automation.
  • Exact alarms — used for the sleep and wake reminders you set yourself.

Every permission is requested at the moment it is first needed, and you can refuse any of them. Refusing a permission disables only the feature that depends on it.

4. Third-party services

Mahan AI sends requests to the following providers. Each receives only what is needed to answer that one request.

  • Google Gemini — primary AI provider. Receives your prompt and, during automation, the list of on-screen element labels.
  • Groq and Sarvam AI — backup AI providers, used only when the primary provider is unavailable. They receive the same request content.
  • Google AdMob — serves the ads that keep the app free. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier. You can reset or delete this identifier in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

We do not control these providers' own data practices. Their policies apply to what they receive: Google, Groq, Sarvam AI.

5. What we never collect

  • We do not create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not upload your photos, videos, files or chat history.
  • We do not read your messages for advertising.
  • We do not sell or rent data to anyone.
  • We do not track your location.

6. Financial and payment apps

Mahan AI includes an App Block feature. Any app you add to that list is excluded from automation entirely — the assistant will not read or tap inside it. We recommend adding all banking, UPI and payment apps there.

7. Children's privacy

Mahan AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it completely:

  • Clear chat history from the app's settings.
  • Clear automation history from the app's settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything.

Requests we send to AI providers are not stored by us. If you want a provider to delete what it received, contact that provider directly.

9. Security

Traffic between the app and our backend uses HTTPS. App-private storage is protected by Android's own sandboxing. No system is perfectly secure, so avoid using automation on screens containing passwords, card numbers or one-time codes.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
